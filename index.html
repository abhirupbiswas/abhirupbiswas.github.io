Interested in

I am a 4th-year BS-MS student at IISER Kolkata, curious about terrestrial planets, exoplanets, and the atmospheres that shape them. I'm also a big fan of nature documentaries, especially those narrated by Sir David Attenborough.

Hey, I am Abhirup Biswas, a 4th year undergraduate student majoring in Physics with a minor in Earth Science at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata. I have always been fascinated about planets as a whole, and so I plan to work on some phenomenal breakthroughs in planetary science.

Currently, I am doing an internship at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad focusing on estimating shock pressure from meteorite impacts. For this study, I also got the opportunity to collaborate with the Planetary Science Laboratory at the Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad.

Previously, I have remotely worked on a project on the development of a code, related to the modelling of a protoplanetary disk, at NISER Bhubaneswar. I have also worked on 21cm Cosmology, at IISER Kolkata, which involved the morphological analysis of ionized and neutral hydrogen bubbles using simulated data, based on the future Square Kilometre Array (SKA) Radio telescope.

Apart from my academic work, I recently taught myself to swim, and thoroughly enjoy going to early morning swimming sessions. Evening jogs have also really helped me to detox myself from academic workload. I occasionally play table tennis, and I am a casual gamer who prefers good, story driven games. (In particular, Hellblade left a deep impact on me, it explores the concept of "psychosis" and what such patients go through daily.)

Projects

A chronological timeline of my projects and research experiences.

21cm Cosmology & Morphological Analysis

Analyzed ionized and neutral hydrogen bubbles using simulated radio array data tailored to the future Square Kilometre Array (SKA) telescope to study cosmic reionization morphology.

Protoplanetary Disk Modeling Code

Learned about protoplanetary disks and its physical and chemical evolution, and how such a setup can be modelled computationally.

Estimation of Shock Pressure from meteorite impact

Explored the techniques of estimating shock pressure from mineral grains in the meteorite or impact site. It involved experimental analysis of the samples such as micro-Raman and micro-FTIR.
